A course AI policy works when students can tell, for each assignment, what is allowed and how to disclose it. Most published examples are written for essay-based courses. These guides are for quantitative courses, where the hard questions are about code, problem sets and showing work.

Each guide gives three sample policies you can copy and edit:

1. **No generative AI on graded work.**
2. **AI for studying, not for submitted work.**
3. **AI allowed with disclosure**, usually on lower-stakes work, with exams kept AI-free.

Each guide also covers the clauses that matter for that discipline and what recent evidence says. When you are ready to write your own, the [free AI policy generator](/ai-policy-generator) produces a ready-to-paste policy in a few minutes.
